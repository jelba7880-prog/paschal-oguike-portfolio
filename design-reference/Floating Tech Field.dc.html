02  Behavior
Depth

Deep objects are smaller, softer and barely move. Foreground objects are larger, sharper and travel furthest. Nothing is blurred enough to read as an effect.

Inertia

Each object chases a target rather than holding one. Move fast and you can see the field trail, overshoot slightly, then settle.

Docking

Scroll position drives one progress value. Cursor influence fades out, rotation settles to zero, scale resolves to the slot — staggered by depth.
